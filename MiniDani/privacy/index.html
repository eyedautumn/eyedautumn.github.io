MiniDani

Privacy Policy

MiniDani runs on your computer. The maintainer does not receive your Roblox credentials, project files, or activity logs through a MiniDani server. The plugin and companion have no maintainer-operated analytics or telemetry.

Roblox sign-in and asset operations

Your browser signs in directly with Roblox. With your consent, the companion uses Roblox OAuth to identify your account, display your profile, and read or upload assets for the accounts you select. Relevant asset IDs, metadata requests, and files you choose to upload go to Roblox or its content delivery services. Roblox handles these requests under its Privacy Policy.

Asset discovery

When deeper asset discovery is needed, the companion may query the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine using public Roblox asset or creator identifiers. These queries do not include your Roblox OAuth token or your local project files. See the Internet Archive privacy terms.

What stays on your device

The Studio plugin communicates with the companion on localhost. The companion stores OAuth credentials in your operating system’s credential store. Preferences, replacement and upload records, timing statistics, diagnostics, and retained downloads stay on your device.

Your controls

Sign out in MiniDani to clear its local OAuth credentials. Revoke the app in Roblox’s authorized apps settings to withdraw access. You can turn off download retention for future successful uploads in MiniDani Settings. You can remove existing downloaded files and other local records from the companion’s data directory. Removing a local record does not delete an asset already uploaded to Roblox.

This website and contact

This static site uses no analytics or tracking cookies of its own. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard connection information as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. For privacy questions, contact EyedAutumn on GitHub.
