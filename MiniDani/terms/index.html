Age requirement

You must be at least 13 years old to use MiniDani. Roblox requires a 13+ account to authorize OAuth 2.0 apps. See the Roblox OAuth 2.0 overview.

Using MiniDani

Use the software only with Roblox accounts and experiences you are authorized to manage. Follow the Roblox Terms of Use, Roblox’s applicable creator rules, and applicable law.

Asset rights

Only download, copy, upload, or replace assets when you have the necessary rights and permissions. Ownership of an experience or the ability to access an asset does not by itself give you the right to re-upload it. Respect the creator’s license and Roblox’s access controls.

Changes to your place and account

You choose the operations you run. Uploads may count toward Roblox quotas, and replacements can change references in your place. Review the selection and keep a backup before bulk changes. Roblox may limit or moderate uploaded content.

MiniDani is provided as available. Roblox API changes, outages, and rate limits may interrupt it. To the extent permitted by law, the software is provided without warranties and the maintainer is not responsible for losses arising from its use. Your mandatory consumer rights remain in effect.

Ending use and getting in touch

You can stop using MiniDani at any time, sign out, and revoke access in Roblox’s authorized apps settings. For questions about these terms, contact EyedAutumn on GitHub.
